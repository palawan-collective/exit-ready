How the readiness score works

Identity verified25 points
Funding path declared25 points
NDA signed20 points
Prior ownership15 points
References15 points

80 and above reads as ready. 55 to 79 is developing. Below 55 is early. Sellers see the number, not just a badge.

What we check, and what we don't

Not a background check and not a guarantee. It answers one question: can this person actually complete a purchase? Identity is checked; funding, deposit and timeline are declared and may be evidenced later.
