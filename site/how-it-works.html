Six stages, both sides verified

An ordinary listing site stops after the enquiry. This one carries the deal all the way to the final payment and keeps a record at every step.

List

Ten minutes of facts: financials, operations, successor type, and the disclosure flags that usually surface too late.

What verification actually checks

Not a background check and not a guarantee. It answers one question: can this person actually complete a purchase?

Identity
A government identity document matched to the person. For an owner, also proof the business exists and that they are entitled to sell it.
25 points
Funding path
How the purchase will be paid for: cash, bank loan, seller financing, an earn-out, or a combination. Declared, with the deposit capacity stated as a band.
25 points
NDA signed
The buyer has accepted the confidentiality terms. This is what unlocks the financial detail.
20 points
Prior ownership
Has run or bought a business before. Not required, but it changes how much transition support the deal needs.
15 points
References
One or more people who can speak to the buyer's conduct. Checked only with the buyer's consent.
15 points
Bands
80 and above reads as ready. 55 to 79 is developing. Below 55 is early. You see the number, not just a badge.
Transparent scoring

A readiness score is not a guarantee

It reports what a buyer has declared and had checked. Deals still fall through. The score narrows the field; your own judgement and your advisers close the gap.

The NDA gate

Your numbers should not be public. They should also not be locked behind a phone call with someone who may never buy.

What stays public
The shape of the deal
Business type, location, asking price band, cash flow band, established year, employee count, premises status, and every disclosure flag.
What waits behind the NDA
The detail
Exact revenue and cost breakdowns, monthly figures, supplier terms, lease documents, and supporting records.
What is logged
Who saw what, when
Every signature and every document release carries a timestamp and an identity. If a buyer shares your numbers, you can see who had them.

Three regions to start

Each has different paperwork. The handoff plan adapts.

United States

Bank financing language, state-level licence transfers, and lease assignment rules that vary by state.

United Kingdom

Leasehold and freehold treated separately. Different licensing regime and a different set of transfer consents.

Philippines

Permit and registration transfers handled through the relevant government offices. Owner-maintained records are common, so the unaudited flag carries more weight.
