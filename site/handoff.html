Twelve milestones, signed off by both sides

Transition support is the most promised and least delivered part of any small business sale. Here it is a list with dates and names against it.

The standard sequence

    How it adapts to the successor

    The same twelve milestones run every deal. What changes is the pace, which milestones carry a payment, and how long the training and support windows run.

    Employee buyout

    • Training period is typically longest, because the buyer is learning ownership rather than the trade.
    • Staged payments are normal. Milestone five and milestone eleven often carry instalments.
    • Staff notification is sensitive: the team is the buyer, so sequencing matters.
    • Post-sale support usually runs longest here. The seller often stays reachable for months.

    Local operator

    • Diligence is fastest. The buyer already knows the trade and often the area.
    • Fewer surprises on suppliers and landlord consent, since relationships may already exist.
    • Training period is short. Handover of accounts and credentials is the bulk of it.
    • Earn-outs are less common; straightforward purchase with a deposit is more typical.

    First-time buyer

    • Financing takes longest. The verification window matters most here.
    • Training runs long, and often covers basic business administration, not just the trade.
    • Licence and permit transfer is more likely to need hand-holding.
    • Post-sale support is essential. Budget for it explicitly rather than promising it verbally.

    What each milestone records

    StatusPending, in progress, done, or blocked
    OwnerWhich side is responsible for moving it
    Due dateAgreed between both sides once an offer is accepted
    CompletionTimestamp, recorded when both sides confirm
    Evidence noteA short reference to what was exchanged or signed

      Why this matters

      When transition support is a sentence in a listing description, there is nothing to hold anyone to. When it is twelve dated milestones with two signatures each, the buyer knows what they are getting and the seller knows when they are free.

      Where payments attach

      Milestone five can carry a deposit into escrow. Milestone eleven can carry the balance release. Earn-outs attach to milestones eight through ten, where retention and trading performance are visible.
